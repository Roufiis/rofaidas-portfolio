01 · Maaliy

Maaliy

Tax deadlines, rebuilt around the client

Discipline
Product design
Platform
Accounting ERP, Arabic-first
User interviews · Information architecture · Dashboard UI · RTL design system · User interviews · Information architecture · Dashboard UI · RTL design system · 

Accountants were losing tax deadlines in a system that had everything and showed nothing. I rebuilt it around the client instead of the operation.

  • Entries, invoices and taxes merged into one client view
  • A summary row that opens into the full client page
  • A deadlines calendar with alerts, the top pain in interviews
  • One RTL design system across 14+ screens and 7 roles
02 · Sadeem

Sadeem

A store dashboard that thinks in Arabic

Discipline
Product design
Platform
Responsive admin panel, RTL
Arabic-first UX · RTL layout · Design system · E-commerce · Arabic-first UX · RTL layout · Design system · E-commerce · 

A single-vendor store dashboard for shop owners who think in Arabic. It reads right to left from the first pixel, not as a mirrored afterthought.

  • State before value: stock is a badge, payment and fulfilment are two states
  • A permission matrix of 8 areas × 5 actions replaced a wall of checkboxes
  • Swappable cards, so owners choose what stays on screen
  • 33 primitives and 84 semantic tokens across 13 screens
03 · Dhi

Dhi

Consult, shop and track a room in one app

Discipline
Product design
Platform
Mobile app, RTL
Competitor analysis · Mobile UX · RTL design system · Saudi market · Competitor analysis · Mobile UX · RTL design system · Saudi market · 

An interior design platform for homes in Saudi Arabia. Competitor apps split consultation, shopping and tracking; Dhi keeps them in one place.

  • A style quiz that gets people to a direction fast
  • In-app project chat with the designer, fewer phone calls
  • 40+ screens on an Arabic RTL design system
