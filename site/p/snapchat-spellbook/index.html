LVL 01 The quest

What if the ghost went to magic school?

XP
what-is-snapchat.txt

A camera that talks

Snapchat is a multimedia messaging app for sharing moments through photos, videos and texts that often disappear after they are viewed. It is one of the most popular social platforms and gives people a playful way to express themselves.

whats-new.exe

New icons, new interface

This hypothetical redesign reimagines Snapchat's visual identity: a fresh set of custom pictograms that match the app's young, dynamic spirit, and a reworked interface that is easier to use, more appealing and more engaging.

Student concept, made for fun. Not affiliated with Snap Inc.

Power-ups · what they love
  • ✦Bright and vibrant color schemes
  • ✦Trendy, modern and dynamic styles
  • ✦Clear, straightforward and easy-to-navigate interfaces
  • ✦Features that encourage creativity and self-expression, such as AR filters and personalized content
  • ✦Smooth and fast performance with minimal lag
Enemies · what they hate
  • ✕Overly complex navigation that takes too many steps to reach a feature
  • ✕Cluttered layouts that feel overwhelming or disorganized
  • ✕Features that are hard to find or understand without guidance
  • ✕Lack of customization, or content that feels impersonal
  • ✕Slow loading times or bugs that disrupt the experience
